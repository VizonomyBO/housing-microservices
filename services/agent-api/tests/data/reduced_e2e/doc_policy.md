# Metro Housing Continuity Memo – Q2 FY25
Prepared by the Metro Housing Department to summarize policy guardrails for the reduced E2E scenario.

## Voucher Expansion
- Emergency voucher program expanded from **three** pilot districts to **five districts** (Districts 2, 4, 7, 8, 9).
- District 9 must reserve 15% of slots for seniors affected by the Harbor bridge retrofit.
- Attachments referencing this section should cite `doc_policy#voucher-expansion`.

## Rent Guardrails
- Rent relief cap lowered to **32% of household income** for any expansion district.
- Landlords participating in rapid placements must waive security deposits above $1,000.
- Audits run quarterly; breaches trigger a 90-day reimbursement freeze.

## Reporting Cadence
- Pilot requires a monthly KPI digest emailed to Ava plus the finance controller.
- Digest must summarize ledger spend, KPI trends, and cite District 9 as the risk focus.
- Failure to send the digest pauses new voucher approvals until compliance restored.
